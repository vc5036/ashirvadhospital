About the hospital

Dental Care Built Around Your Comfort

Shri Aashirvad Dental Hospital is equipped with advanced equipment and follows modern dental treatment techniques. Every treatment plan begins with a careful understanding of the patient's concern.

Patient comfort and convenience are a priority. Sterilization is carried out using a modern B Class Autoclave Sterilizer, and the campus offers a spacious common waiting hall, a special VIP room, in-house parking and a solar power plant.

  • Modern Treatment Techniques
  • B Class Autoclave Sterilization
  • Spacious Waiting Hall
