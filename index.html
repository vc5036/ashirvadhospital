About Us

Modern Dentistry With Patient Comfort at Its Heart

Shri Aashirvad Dental Hospital is built, staffed and equipped with advanced dental equipment and modern treatment techniques, so every visit feels calm, clear and well cared for.

We take consultations and physical examinations seriously, giving each patient the time needed to understand their treatment before it begins. Every instrument used in our clinic is sterilized with a modern, state-of-the-art B Class Autoclave Sterilizer, and patient comfort is prioritized at every step of the treatment journey.

Director

Dr. Sushil Kumar

BDS, MHA — Director, Shri Aashirvad Dental Hospital

With 19 years of clinical experience, Dr. Sushil Kumar has built Shri Aashirvad Dental Hospital around a simple idea: dentistry should be thorough, modern and centered on the patient in the chair.

He believes in taking the time to understand each case before treating it, using modern dental practices and equipment to guide every decision, and holding the hospital to a consistently high standard of quality care.
